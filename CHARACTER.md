# Miso

Miso is a fictional adult ginger tabby and Freelance Cat. Main claim: “Hire me. I'm very employable.” Secondary line: “Available for meaningful work. And boxes.”

## Visual authority
The approved generated hero portrait at public/images/hero.webp establishes Miso. Original and generation provenance are recorded in docs/ASSETS.md. Stable traits: amber eyes, cream muzzle and chest bib, pink nose, orange forehead M, dark orange stripes, white front toes (not full white legs), striped tail with rounded tip, believable adult proportions. User-provided cat photographs later become the visual authority.

## Setting
Sunny home studio, oak desk, silver unbranded laptop, plain cardboard boxes, beige linen sofa, warm white walls, one green plant and a sunny window. Natural fur tones and ordinary cat anatomy. No suits or human hands.

## Voice
First person. Confident, mildly demanding, curious, affectionate on my own terms. Ordinary behavior is specialist work. Short specific observations; never baby talk or incessant cat puns.

“I inspect boxes, warm keyboards, and bring a strong presence to meetings I was not invited to.”
“My working hours depend on the sun. The sun has not shared its calendar.”
“I have never missed a deadline I understood.”

Object references and case studies are unmistakable fiction. Never invent human endorsements, real animal availability, owner's details, real company clients, or live demand.
