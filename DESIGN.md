# Visual identity

The user's committed world takes precedence over generated design alternatives: a photographic freelancer portfolio with pop-editorial confidence. A giant ordinary cat beside a serious professional claim is the central joke. No speculative direction/approval round was needed because the supplied brief specifies identity, fonts, palette, layout, imagery and user flow.

## System
- Bricolage Grotesque Variable for wordmark and headings, Hanken Grotesk Variable for interface/body. Local licensed WOFF2, predictable loading.
- Semantic CSS tokens: --sky #BEDCED, --butter #F6E7A7, --paper #FFFCF4, --tomato #D8432D, --ink #24231F. Contrast-adjusted --action #C73522 and --accent-text #AA2C1B support small button/label text. Final measured ratios are in docs/QA.md.
- Broad blue hero and final invitation; paper reading/form areas; butter reserved for personality/interview. No alternating color stripes as filler.
- Max width 1280px, 64px desktop side space scaling to 20px mobile. Generous section gaps, thin ink rules, big type and deliberate photo scale. Mobile recomposes hero with cat visible beside/below short text.
- Small original M/paw-inspired SVG mark, typographic miso. wordmark, functional SVG icons. One rounded approval seal, occasional angled photographic label. No emoji imagery, continuous wobble, generic dashboard cards, gradients, or decorative paw trails.
- Photograph library follows CHARACTER.md. Transparent hero retains actual alpha and has a 480px mobile derivative selected through a native picture source; work photos use clean landscape crops and captions. Natural fur colors and correct intrinsic dimensions.

## Surfaces and states
Homepage/work are Experience/Persuade; about/resume/terms are Read; offer flow is Operate. Forms use native fields with visible labels, errors, summaries and stable controls. Step headings receive focus on progression. Destructive choices use browser confirmation dialogs. Public pages do not wait for local hydration.

Focus: 3px visible outline with offset. Buttons have pointer/hover/disabled feedback. Links underline on hover; selected nav has a visible underline. Accepted stamp enters once; reduced motion removes movement and smooth scrolling. No automatic sound.

## Responsive
360–390, 768, 1024 and 1440px are verification targets. Header becomes a keyboard-accessible mobile menu; offer history remains secondary. Work columns collapse; offer form is one column on phones. Long names wrap in UI and exported cards.
